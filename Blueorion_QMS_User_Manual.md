# Blueorion QMS User Manual

## 1. Introduction & System Overview
Blueorion QMS (Quality Management System) is a web-based platform designed to streamline recruitment, applicant tracking, staff monitoring, and compliance for BLUEORION Recruitment Services Corp. This manual provides step-by-step guidance for staff, admins, and applicants.

## 2. Login & Access
- Visit the QMS portal: https://blueorion-qms.onrender.com/
- Click the **Login** button.
- Enter your username and password.
- If you forget your password, contact your system administrator.

## 3. Dashboard Overview
- After login, you’ll see the **Command Center Dashboard**.
- Key panels:
  - **Applicant Lifecycle Tracker**: View and manage applicant progress.
  - **Staff Monitoring & Approval**: Approve or monitor staff actions.
  - **Backup & Restore**: Access data backup features.

## 4. Applicant Management
- Add new applicants via the **Applicant Forms**.
- Track applicant status in the **Lifecycle Tracker**.
- Export applicant data as needed (CSV/JSON).

## 5. Staff Monitoring & Approval
- Access the **Staff Monitoring Panel** from the dashboard.
- Approve, reject, or comment on staff actions.
- View logs of recent staff activities.

## 6. Data Backup & Restore
- Use the **Backup** button to save current data to the `qms_safe_zone` directory.
- Restore previous backups from the **Backup Management** section.
- Always verify backup completion in the logs.

## 7. Troubleshooting & Support
- If the dashboard hangs, perform a hard refresh (Ctrl+F5).
- For login issues, ensure your credentials are correct.
- For data discrepancies, check the backup logs and contact IT support.

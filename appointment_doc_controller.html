POSITION: Document Controller – Malate Office

REFERENCE: DMW-QMS Framework, D.O. No. 03, Series of 2025  |  QMS Manual Section 1.5

You are hereby designated as the Document Controller for Blueorion Recruitment Services Corp, effective April 15, 2026. You are responsible for maintaining the integrity of all digital and physical records across our 17-System Quality Management System.

Key Duties:

  • Master Document List: Maintain and update the Master List of Documents in System #8 (Expenses / Doc Control), ensuring all records are version-controlled and dated.
  • Worker File Integrity: Ensure all worker Bio-Datas, Medical Certificates, and Contracts are correctly uploaded to the QMS backend before any deployment is processed.
  • Form Currency: Verify that only the latest versions of DMW-approved forms are in use by recruitment and welfare staff. Obsolete forms must be removed immediately.
  • Document Retention: Safeguard physical and digital files in accordance with the NPC Data Retention Policy; destroy expired records through approved disposal procedures only.
  • Audit Readiness: Ensure all document folders are organized and accessible for DMW Licensing and Regulation Bureau (LRB) inspection at any time.
  • Reporting: Report any missing, damaged, or non-conforming documents to the Quality Management Representative (QMR) immediately.

This designation is effective from the date signed below and is subject to annual review by the Quality Management Representative.

📅 Internal Audit Plan — QMS-DMW Compliance
Agency Name:Blueorion Recruitment Services Corp. Audit Period: Lead Auditor:Quality Management Representative (QMR) — Genevie / Lyndie B. Jamias Document Ref:BOR-QMS-AUD-2026-01
1. Objective

To evaluate the effectiveness of the agency's Quality Management System (QMS) and ensure full alignment with the 2026 DMW Rules and Regulations and ethical recruitment standards as defined by the IOM Ethical Recruitment Framework.

2. Scope of Audit
🏢 Administration & Finance
License maintenance, Escrow, Social Media registry
👤 Recruitment & Processing
Candidate screening, MWO-verified contracts, No Placement Fee compliance
🌍 Welfare & Monitoring
OFW check-ins, OWMS logs, contingency planning
⚖️ Legal & Case Management
DMW Portal responses, grievance handling
3. Audit Schedule & Assignment

4. Audit Methodology
📁
Document Review
Checking the Master List and individual worker folders
🗣️
Interview
Asking staff how they handle specific scenarios (e.g., emergency calls)
👁️
Observation
Watching actual encoding into the DMW portal in real time
🔗
Traceability
Tracking one random worker from application through to deployment
5. Non-Conformity (NC) Classification
🔴 Major NC
A direct violation of DMW rules (e.g., missing contract verification, no placement fee evidence). Requires Immediate Corrective Action before the next audit phase.
🟠 Minor NC
A lapse in internal procedure that does not directly violate law but affects quality and readiness. Requires a Corrective Action Plan within 30 days.
💡 QMR Recommendation — Mock Audit Protocol
Have your QMR run a Mock Audit on 10 random worker files using the DMW Checklist before Day 1. If more than 2 files have missing logs or unrecorded check-ins, schedule a team retraining session before the official audit date.

⚠️ DMW Audit Tip — Preponderance of Evidence
The DMW's 2026 adjudication rules rely heavily on "Preponderance of Evidence." A missing document may automatically rule in favor of a claimant.

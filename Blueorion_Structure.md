# Blueorion Structure

This document is the master plan for the Blueorion Recruitment Management System.

## 12 QMS Sub-Systems

1. Welfare & Monitoring
   - Track worker status abroad and communication logs.
2. Sourcing
   - Manage recruitment leads and manpower pooling.
3. Complaint & Grievance
   - Log disputes and legal resolutions for compliance.
4. Management & Leadership
   - Executive oversight and agency decision-making logs.
5. Resource & Competence
   - Manage staff training and internal agency resources.
6. Audit & Improvement
   - Internal audits and corrections for ISO/DMW standards.
7. Document Control
   - Central hub for all QMS manuals and official letters.
8. Payment & Voucher
   - Financial tracking of recruitment-related expenses.
9. Profile & Contact
   - The master database of all applicants and candidates.
10. Selection & CV
    - Sorting candidates and matching them to job orders.
11. Contract & Re-engagement
    - Managing finished contracts and returning workers.
12. FRA System
    - Managing Foreign Recruitment Agency partners and job orders.

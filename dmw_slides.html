Scope of the Quality Management System

ISO 9001:2015 Clause 4.3 | DMW-QMS Framework

The QMS of Blueorion covers all processes related to:

  • ✓Applicant sourcing & lead management
  • ✓Screening, selection & job matching
  • ✓Pre-employment documentation
  • ✓OEC, OWWA, TESDA, medical processing
  • ✓FRA coordination & job orders
  • ✓Worker deployment & flight coordination
  • ✓Post-deployment welfare monitoring
  • ✓Complaint & grievance handling
  • ✓Document control & records management
  • ✓Internal audits & management review
Exclusion (ISO 8.3): Design and Development — excluded as Blueorion does not design new service products. Services are defined by DMW standards and employer job orders.

Internal Audit & Continual Improvement

ISO 9001:2015 Clauses 9.2 & 10 | System 07

Audit Schedule

  • 4 full QMS audits per year (quarterly)
  • Auditors independent of audited functions
  • Findings: Major NC / Minor NC / Observation
  • All NCs tracked until closure & verification
  • Management Review quarterly (chaired by President)

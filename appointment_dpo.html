APPOINTMENT LETTER: DATA PROTECTION OFFICER (DPO)

DATE: April 15, 2026

TO: Emmanuel Carbonilla

POSITION: Data Protection Officer

REFERENCE: Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012)  |  NPC Registration Requirement  |  QMS Manual Section 1.6

In compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (R.A. 10173) and the regulations of the National Privacy Commission (NPC), you are hereby appointed as the Data Protection Officer (DPO) for Blueorion Recruitment Services Corp, effective April 15, 2026. You will oversee the agency's full compliance with privacy laws and the security of all personal data held in our digital and physical records.

Primary Responsibilities:

  • NPC Registration: Register the agency's personal data processing systems with the National Privacy Commission and maintain the registration annually.
  • Privacy Compliance: Ensure all worker personally identifiable information (PII) — passports, contracts, medical records, bank details — is collected, stored, and disposed of in strict accordance with R.A. 10173.
  • Access Control: Oversee the level-based access system in the QMS (President / Encoder / Welfare-LO roles) to prevent unauthorized access to worker data.
  • Breach Response: Lead the agency's response to any personal data breach, notifying affected individuals and the NPC within 72 hours of discovery.
  • Privacy Impact Assessments: Conduct Privacy Impact Assessments (PIAs) before implementing new data processing systems or recruitment workflows.
  • Staff Training: Conduct annual data privacy awareness training for all Malate and Makati staff.
  • Coordination: Act as the primary liaison between the agency and the NPC on all data privacy matters.
🔒 NPC Reminder: Under NPC Circular No. 17-01, agencies processing personal data of 1,000 or more data subjects are required to register their data processing systems and formally designate a DPO. This appointment letter serves as the formal designation required for NPC compliance.

This appointment is effective from the date signed below and shall remain in force until revoked in writing by the President or superseded by a new appointment.

JOB DESCRIPTION: DOCUMENT CONTROLLER

DepartmentDocument Control / Operations Support Reports ToQuality Management Representative OfficeMalate Headquarters EffectivityApril 15, 2026 ReferenceDMW QMS Framework, D.O. No. 03, Series of 2025

The Document Controller is responsible for keeping the Blueorion digital and physical records complete, current, readable, and properly filed. This role ensures that the agency's document environment remains orderly and inspection-ready.

Key Responsibilities
  • Data Entry: Manage the flow of applicant records from Blueorionapply@yahoo.com into the Selection Table (System #11), ensuring complete and timely entry.
  • Verification: Check that every uploaded PDF, including medical results and passport copies, is clear, readable, and valid before records are marked complete.
  • Version Control: Ensure the office uses only the latest DMW-standard employment contracts, forms, and approved templates.
  • Archive Management: Keep digital files backed up and organized within the VS Code environment and document storage structure.
  • Master List Maintenance: Update System #8 with current document references, revision status, and effective dates.
  • Release Control: Remove obsolete forms from circulation and report any unauthorized or outdated templates to the QMR.
Authority

The Document Controller is authorized to reject incomplete uploads, hold document packages for correction, and request re-submission when records do not meet Blueorion document standards.

Performance Standard

All worker files should be legible, current, and retrievable on demand. No outdated DMW form should remain in operational use.

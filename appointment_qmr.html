POSITION: Quality Management Representative

REFERENCE: DMW-QMS Framework, D.O. No. 03, Series of 2025

In accordance with the DMW-QMS Framework (Department Order No. 03, Series of 2025), you are hereby appointed as the Quality Management Representative (QMR) for Blueorion Recruitment Services Corp, effective immediately.

Primary Responsibilities:

  • QMS Oversight: Ensure that the 17-system Quality Management System is established, implemented, and maintained in accordance with ISO 9001 and DMW standards.
  • Compliance Reporting: Report directly to the President on the performance of the QMS and any need for improvement.
  • Liaison: Act as the primary contact person for the DMW Licensing and Regulation Bureau (LRB) during audits and inspection visits.
  • Document Control: Supervise the Document Controller to ensure all records (Selection, Medical, Welfare) are audit-ready at all times.
  • Internal Audits: Schedule and oversee quarterly internal audits using the agency's digital QMS (System #7 – Audit & Improvement).
  • Corrective Actions: Track open non-conformities and confirm closure within the target period.

This appointment is effective from the date signed below and shall remain in force until revoked in writing by the President.

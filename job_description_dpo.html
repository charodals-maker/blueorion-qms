JOB DESCRIPTION: DATA PROTECTION OFFICER (DPO)

DepartmentData Privacy / Compliance Reports ToPresident & Owner OfficeAtlantis Beacon Tower Office EffectivityApril 15, 2026 ReferenceR.A. 10173 / NPC Compliance / DMW Structural Foundation

The Data Protection Officer (DPO) protects Blueorion from legal and operational risk by ensuring that worker, staff, and partner data is processed in accordance with the Data Privacy Act and National Privacy Commission requirements.

Key Responsibilities
  • Access Control: Ensure staff only see the records and systems necessary for their role, in line with the Blueorion access firewall and permission structure.
  • Privacy Compliance: Manage and maintain National Privacy Commission registration and privacy obligations for the Atlantis Beacon Tower office.
  • Breach Protocol: Lead response actions when worker data may be compromised, including containment, reporting, documentation, and notification.
  • Policy Enforcement: Monitor use of worker data in Selection, Profile, Welfare, and Document Control systems to ensure lawful processing and confidentiality.
  • Training and Awareness: Orient staff on privacy obligations, password handling, restricted access, and proper sharing of sensitive documents.
  • Record Review: Audit privacy-sensitive workflows and recommend corrective action whenever exposure risks are identified.
Authority

The DPO is authorized to suspend unsafe data handling practices, require corrective action for privacy violations, and escalate serious incidents directly to the President.

Performance Standard

No unauthorized access incident should go undocumented. Privacy controls, user access, and breach records must remain current and defensible during inspection.

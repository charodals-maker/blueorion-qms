JOB DESCRIPTION: QUALITY MANAGEMENT REPRESENTATIVE (QMR)

DepartmentManagement / Quality Management System Reports ToPresident & Owner OfficeMalate Headquarters EffectivityApril 15, 2026 ReferenceDMW QMS Framework, D.O. No. 03, Series of 2025

The Quality Management Representative (QMR) serves as the bridge between Blueorion leadership and daily operations. This role ensures that the 17-system Quality Management System is functioning, monitored, and ready for DMW inspection at all times.

Key Responsibilities
  • System Integrity: Monitor all 17 systems to ensure data is entered daily, records are complete, and each operational board reflects current status.
  • Audit Readiness: Maintain and organize the Risk & Opportunity Register and all QMS evidence required for DMW inspections.
  • Management Review: Meet with the President monthly to discuss agency red-flags, deployment delays, compliance gaps, and corrective actions.
  • Training: Ensure all new staff understand the Blueorion Quality Policy, document control rules, and their responsibilities inside the QMS.
  • Corrective Action Oversight: Track open findings in System #7 and follow through until closure and verification.
  • Cross-System Coordination: Align Selection (#11), Deployment (#14), Welfare (#15), and Document Control (#8) so audit evidence remains consistent.
Authority

The QMR is authorized to request updates from operational staff, escalate non-conformities to management, and require corrective actions when records or workflows fall below Blueorion QMS standards.

Performance Standard

All major systems must remain inspection-ready, all red-flags must be tracked with action owners, and monthly management review summaries must be documented for leadership review.

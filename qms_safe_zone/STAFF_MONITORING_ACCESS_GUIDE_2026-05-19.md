## 📋 Step-by-Step: How to View Candidate Monitoring

### Step 1: Login
1. Open https://blueorion-qms-backend.onrender.com/login.html
2. Enter your staff username
3. Enter your password
4. Click **LOGIN TO DASHBOARD**

### Step 2: Open Monitoring Workstation
1. After login, open https://blueorion-qms-backend.onrender.com/staff_workstation.html
2. Or from Dashboard, click **Open Workstation →** button

### Step 3: Access Real-Time Candidate Monitoring
In the Staff Workstation menu, click one of these:

#### **Option A: QMS Candidate Tracking (HSW vs Skilled)**
- View separated Household Worker (HSW) track
- View separate Skilled Worker track
- See all 6 core compliance milestones
- Track: Selection → Medical Test → NC2/Certificates → Biometrics → PDOS → Deployment

#### **Option B: Applicant Lifecycle Tracker**
- View official candidate records (56 total)
- See current milestone status for each candidate
- Search by name or position
- Filter by status

#### **Option C: Biometric / Medical Monitoring**
- View medical test status in real-time
- See biometric scheduling
- Track medical holds and clearances

## 📊 What You Can See (View-Only Access)

✅ **READ & VIEW:**
- Candidate names, ages, contact numbers
- Current QMS status and milestone
- Medical test results and dates
- Biometric records
- Deployment schedules
- FRA employer assignments
- Selection pipeline status

❌ **CANNOT DO (Admin-Only):**
- Edit candidate records
- Approve or reject submissions
- Modify medical status
- Change deployment dates
- Delete or archive records

## 🚨 Troubleshooting

**"Page not loading" or "503 error"**
1. Wait 60 seconds
2. Press Ctrl+F5 (hard refresh)
3. Log out and log in again

**"Access Denied" message**
1. Check that you are logged in as staff (not guest)
2. Contact your QMR or Document Controller

**"Candidates not showing up"**
1. Refresh the page (F5)
2. Check the Applicant Lifecycle Tracker section
3. Ensure the search filter is cleared

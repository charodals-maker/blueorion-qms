Company Profile

Who We Are

Blueorion Recruitment Services Corp helps Filipino workers pursue legitimate overseas and local employment opportunities through structured sourcing, application review, document validation, and coordinated worker support.

Hiring Advisory

Before applying, please use only Blueorion's official channels and verify every job instruction with our office. We do not recognize recruitment instructions sent from unverified personal accounts pretending to represent the company.

Official Process

Use the official online application form

Applicants should submit CV, photo, and passport details only through the official application page and confirmed company contact details.

Important Reminder

Never pay to unknown personal accounts

Do not send placement, processing, or document fees to unverified bank accounts, e-wallets, or personal representatives without formal company confirmation.

Worker Protection

Prepare only authentic documents

All identification, passport, licenses, and employment records must be current, accurate, and personally owned by the applicant.

Anti-Illegal Recruitment Notice
If any person claims to represent Blueorion and asks for money, documents, or travel commitments outside our official process, report it immediately through our verified office contacts.

Company Introduction

Blueorion Recruitment Services Corp. is a Department of Migrant Workers (DMW) licensed overseas employment agency established in 2002. Headquartered at Atlantis Beacon Tower, Malate, Manila, the company has over two decades of experience placing Filipino workers across 12 countries in the Middle East, East Asia, and Southeast Asia. The company operates under full DMW supervision and is committed to ethical, professional, and welfare-first recruitment practices.

Mission

To provide ethical, transparent, and professional overseas recruitment services that uphold the rights, dignity, and welfare of every Filipino worker, in full compliance with Philippine labor laws, DMW regulations, and international quality standards.

Vision

To be the most trusted and compliant overseas recruitment agency in the Philippines — known for zero illegal recruitment, excellence in OFW welfare, and innovation in digital QMS management.

  • Compliance — Strict adherence to DMW, OWWA, NPC, and ISO 9001:2015 standards.

3. Scope of the QMS (ISO 4.3)

Scope Statement

The Quality Management System of Blueorion Recruitment Services Corp. covers all processes and activities related to the sourcing, screening, selection, documentation processing, deployment, and post-deployment welfare monitoring of Filipino overseas workers. This QMS applies to all staff, all service delivery processes, and all information systems used in licensed overseas recruitment operations, in full compliance with DMW D.O. No. 03, Series of 2025 and ISO 9001:2015.

Processes Included in Scope

  • Recruitment sourcing, lead generation, and manpower pooling
  • Applicant screening, evaluation, and job matching to employer requirements
  • Pre-employment documentation (passport, OEC, OWWA registration, medical exam, TESDA certification)
  • Coordination with Foreign Recruitment Agencies (FRA) and management of job orders
  • Worker deployment processing, flight coordination, and airport assistance
  • Post-deployment welfare monitoring and deployed worker status tracking
  • Welfare complaint and grievance handling in coordination with DMW and Philippine embassies
  • Internal document control, records management, and QMS documentation
  • Internal audits, corrective actions, and management review
  • Staff training, competence management, and human resource administration
  • Financial expense management, payment vouchers, and payroll processing
  • Worker contract management, re-engagement, and contract renewal
  • Data privacy compliance under Republic Act 10173

Exclusion (ISO 8.3 — Design and Development)

ISO 9001:2015 Clause 8.3 (Design and Development) is excluded. Blueorion does not design or develop new recruitment service products. All services are defined by DMW regulatory standards and employer job orders. This exclusion does not affect the ability to meet customer requirements or achieve regulatory compliance.

9.2 Internal Audit (ISO 9.2)

Internal QMS audits are conducted quarterly (minimum 4 per year). Auditors are independent of the processes they audit. The Annual Internal Audit Schedule (BLU-AUD-SCH) is approved by the President at the start of each year. Audit findings are classified as Major NC, Minor NC, or Observation, and tracked in System 07 until closure.

Audit Finding Classifications:
• Major Non-Conformity: Absence or systemic failure of a QMS requirement; immediate risk. Corrective action plan required within 7 calendar days.
• Minor Non-Conformity: Partial or isolated failure. Corrective action plan required within 30 calendar days.
• Observation: Potential future risk or improvement area. Addressed at next Management Review.

9.3 Management Review (ISO 9.3)

The President chairs a Management Review meeting at minimum quarterly. Mandatory agenda items include: previous meeting actions, quality objectives performance, customer/worker feedback, audit results, process performance, resource needs, risk register updates, and improvement opportunities. All decisions and actions are documented in Management Review Minutes (BLU-MRM series) and implemented within agreed timelines.

12. Document Control Procedures (ISO 7.5)

Purpose

To ensure all QMS documents and records are properly created, reviewed, approved, issued, revised, and disposed of in a controlled manner — preventing the use of outdated, unauthorized, or incomplete documents in recruitment operations.

Document Categories and Retention

LevelDocument TypeExamplesRetention Period
Level 1 — PolicyQMS Manual, Quality PolicyBLU-QMS-MAN, BLU-QPPermanent; all versions archived
Level 2 — ProceduresQMS procedures, process documentsBLU-PROC seriesCurrent + 2 previous versions
Level 3 — Work InstructionsStep-by-step operational guidesBLU-WI seriesCurrent version only
Level 4 — Records & FormsCompleted forms, checklists, logsBLU-FRM seriesMinimum 3 years (or DMW requirement, whichever is longer)
External DocumentsRegulations, ISO standards, OWWA rules—Current version only; update upon any regulatory change

Document Numbering System

Format: BLU-[TYPE]-[YEAR]-[SEQ]
Example: BLU-QMS-MAN-2026-001 = Blueorion | QMS Manual | 2026 | Document #001
Common prefixes: MAN (manual), PROC (procedure), WI (work instruction), FRM (form), CAR (corrective action report), MRM (management review minutes), TRN (training record), ASR (approved supplier register), MDL (master document list)

Document Lifecycle (Six-Step Process)

  1. Creation: Process owner or QMR creates document using the approved template with document number, version, effective date, and document owner.
  2. Review: QMR reviews for QMS compliance and accuracy. Technical review by relevant process owner.
  3. Approval: Approved by President (Level 1–2) or QMR (Level 3–4) with signature and effective date.
  4. Distribution: Document Controller registers in Master Document List (BLU-MDL-2026) and distributes to staff. Paper copies are stamped CONTROLLED COPY. Uncontrolled copies are stamped FOR INFORMATION ONLY.
  5. Revision: Revisions follow the same review and approval cycle. Version number is incremented. Previous version is archived in read-only format.
  6. Disposal: Superseded documents are removed from active circulation, marked OBSOLETE, and shredded if physical. Digital copies are archived in a secure read-only folder.

Master Document List (BLU-MDL-2026)

The Document Controller maintains the Master Document List updated monthly. It records: document number, title, version, effective date, document owner, next review date, and distribution list. The MDL is available on the Blueorion QMS platform and is reviewed in full during every quarterly internal audit.

13. Welfare Monitoring & OFW Protection

"Blueorion Recruitment Services Corp. places the welfare, dignity, and rights of every Filipino worker above all commercial interests. We maintain active welfare monitoring for all deployed workers and are committed to responding to any welfare concern within 24 hours of receipt."

Welfare Monitoring System (QMS System 03)

All workers deployed through Blueorion are tracked in real-time via the digital Welfare Monitoring System. Records include: full name, destination country, employer, contract dates, OWWA number, contact details, next-of-kin information, and current welfare status. Status categories and required actions:

StatusDefinitionRequired Action
Active / WellWorker is employed normally; no reported concernsRoutine check-in record every 6 months
Concern FlaggedMinor welfare concern reported; under active monitoringWelfare Officer contacts worker within 24 hours; log all communications
Critical / EmergencyAbuse, non-payment, illegal detention, medical emergency, or threat to lifeImmediate escalation to President; coordinate with DMW, Philippine embassy, and OWWA within 2 hours; initiate emergency response protocol (BLU-ERP-2026)
Contract CompletedEmployment contract ended; worker returned to PhilippinesUpdate welfare record; offer re-engagement if applicable
Repatriated EarlyWorker returned before contract end due to termination or emergencyDocument reason; coordinate OWWA emergency repatriation benefits if applicable

Complaint Handling Procedure (QMS System 04)

  1. Receipt: Complaint logged in System 04 within the same business day with reference number (BLU-CMP series).
  2. Acknowledgment: Welfare Officer contacts the worker or family within 24 hours of receipt.
  3. Classification: Complaint classified by urgency level — Critical / High / Medium / Low.
  4. Investigation: Welfare Officer contacts employer and/or FRA; coordinates with Philippine embassy if overseas intervention is required.
  5. Resolution: Action taken per findings: mediation, contract renegotiation, assisted repatriation, or formal referral to DMW.
  6. Closure & Record: Complaint formally closed with resolution summary; reported to QMR and President; filed in System 04.

Anti-Illegal Recruitment Policy

  • Absolute prohibition on collection of placement fees exceeding legal limits set by DMW
  • Anti-illegal recruitment advisory prominently displayed on all Blueorion public platforms and office
  • All staff trained annually on R.A. 8042 (as amended) and DMW anti-illegal recruitment guidelines
  • All job orders verified against current DMW FRA accreditation before any applicant referral
  • All applicants provided written notice of rights before any processing or documentation begins

14. Corrective Action & Non-Conformity (ISO 10.2)

Purpose and Scope

This procedure applies to all non-conformities detected within the Blueorion QMS — whether identified through internal audits, management review, worker welfare incidents, DMW inspection findings, customer complaints, or staff reports. The objective is to eliminate root causes and prevent recurrence of all QMS deficiencies.

Non-Conformity Classification

ClassificationDefinitionResponse Deadline
Major Non-ConformityComplete absence or systemic failure of a QMS requirement; actual or potential risk to worker welfare, legal compliance, or DMW license standingCorrective action plan submitted within 7 calendar days
Minor Non-ConformityPartial or isolated failure of a QMS requirement; non-compliance with documented procedure but no immediate risk to workers or complianceCorrective action plan submitted within 30 calendar days
ObservationPotential future risk or area for improvement; not a current non-conformity but warrants attention and monitoringAddressed at next Management Review meeting

Corrective Action Register — CAR Record Format (System 07)

FieldDescription
CAR NumberBLU-CAR-[YEAR]-[SEQ] (e.g., BLU-CAR-2026-001)
Date DetectedDate the non-conformity was identified or reported
SourceInternal audit / customer complaint / welfare incident / DMW finding / management review / staff report
Non-Conformity DescriptionClear, factual description of what was observed (not opinions)
ClassificationMajor NC / Minor NC / Observation
Root Cause (5-Why or Fishbone)Identified underlying cause — not the symptom
Corrective Action TakenSpecific action, responsible person, and target completion date
Verification of EffectivenessQMR confirmation that the action worked; date verified; objective evidence cited
StatusOpen / In Progress / Closed

Root Cause Analysis Methods

  • 5-Why Analysis: Ask "Why?" five times progressively. Used for most non-conformities. Fast, simple, effective for process failures.
  • Fishbone / Ishikawa Diagram: Used for complex or systemic NCs involving multiple causes across People, Process, Equipment, Environment, Materials, and Measurement.
  • Pareto Analysis: Used when multiple recurring NCs exist to identify and prioritize the most impactful corrective actions.
Escalation Rule — Repeat Non-Conformities: Any non-conformity that recurs within 6 months of CAR closure is automatically reclassified one severity level higher (Observation → Minor NC → Major NC) and requires a mandatory Management Review agenda item with President's direct involvement.

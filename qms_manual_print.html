3. Scope of the QMS

Scope Statement

The Quality Management System of Blueorion Recruitment Services Corp. covers all processes related to the sourcing, screening, selection, documentation, deployment, and post-deployment welfare monitoring of Filipino overseas workers, in compliance with DMW licensing requirements and ISO 9001:2015.

Included in Scope

  • Recruitment sourcing and applicant lead management
  • Applicant screening, selection, and job matching
  • Pre-employment documentation (passport, OEC, medical, TESDA, OWWA)
  • Coordination with Foreign Recruitment Agencies (FRA)
  • Worker deployment processing and flight coordination
  • Post-deployment welfare monitoring and complaint handling
  • Document control and records management
  • Internal audits, corrective actions, and management review
  • Staff competence, training, and resource management
  • Financial expense management and payroll processing
  • Contract management and worker re-engagement

Exclusions

Clause 8.3 (Design and Development) is excluded as Blueorion does not design or develop new recruitment service products — services are defined by DMW standards and employer job orders.

4–7. Organizational Context, Leadership, Planning & Support

4. Context of the Organization (ISO 4.0)

Blueorion has identified its internal and external issues that may affect QMS performance through an annual SWOT analysis and stakeholder mapping exercise (documented in BLU-CTX-001). Key interested parties include: deployed workers, foreign employers, DMW, OWWA, TESDA, NPC, and Philippine embassies in host countries.

5. Leadership & Commitment (ISO 5.0)

Top management, led by Charo Dailo Alaasdi (President), demonstrates leadership by approving the Quality Policy, chairing Management Review meetings, allocating resources, and signing all QMS appointments. The QMR (Lyndie B. Jamias) is formally appointed to oversee day-to-day QMS implementation (Ref: BLU-QMR-APPT-2026).

6. Planning (ISO 6.0)

Risks and opportunities are identified and addressed through the Risk & Opportunity Register (BLU-ROR-2026). Quality objectives with measurable targets are established for each function. Change management procedures ensure that any modifications to QMS processes are planned, reviewed, and documented.

7. Support (ISO 7.0)

  • 7.1 Resources: Adequate staffing, office infrastructure, and digital QMS system are maintained.
  • 7.2 Competence: All staff have defined job descriptions and undergo annual QMS orientation. Training records are maintained in System #5 (Resource & Competence).
  • 7.3 Awareness: Staff are aware of the Quality Policy, their contribution to QMS, and the consequences of non-compliance.
  • 7.4 Communication: Internal communication via staff meetings (monthly) and the Blueorion QMS digital dashboard. External communication through DMW-compliant channels.
  • 7.5 Documented Information: All QMS documents are controlled through System #8 (Document Control). Master list maintained with version history, issuance dates, and review cycles.

8. Operation — Recruitment Process (ISO 8.0)

The core operational process follows a defined recruitment lifecycle:

SOURCING → SCREENING → SELECTION → DOCUMENTATION → MEDICAL / TESDA / OWWA → DEPLOYMENT → POST-DEPLOYMENT WELFARE

Each stage is documented in the corresponding system module and subject to quality verification before proceeding to the next stage.

9–10. Performance Evaluation & Improvement

9. Performance Evaluation (ISO 9.0)

9.1 Monitoring, Measurement & Analysis

Blueorion monitors QMS performance through the digital dashboard system (https://blueorion-qms.onrender.com) which provides real-time KPIs including: total applicants, deployment rate, open complaints, audit items, and document compliance. Reports are generated monthly by the QMR.

9.2 Internal Audit

Internal audits are conducted quarterly by the QMR and designated auditors. Audit findings are categorized as Major Non-Conformity, Minor Non-Conformity, or Observation. All findings are tracked in System #7 (Audit & Improvement) until closure and verification. Auditors are independent of the functions they audit.

9.3 Management Review

The President chairs a Management Review meeting at minimum quarterly. Inputs include: audit results, customer feedback, process performance, resource needs, and risk register updates. All outputs (decisions and actions) are recorded in Management Review Minutes.

10. Improvement (ISO 10.0)

10.1 General

Blueorion continually improves QMS effectiveness through corrective actions, process re-engineering, staff training, and technology upgrades.

10.2 Non-Conformity & Corrective Action

10.3 Continual Improvement

Annual improvement projects are identified from Management Review outputs and tracked using the Improvement Register. The Blueorion digital QMS platform (Node.js / cloud-hosted) is itself a continual improvement initiative, automating compliance tracking and reducing manual paperwork.
